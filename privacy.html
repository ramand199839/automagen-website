Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

AutomaBuild ("I", "me") builds and sets up automations for small businesses, such as the Lead Catcher workflow for n8n. This page explains what data is used, why, and what choices you have.

1. Information you send me

If you contact me by email, WhatsApp or the form on this website, I receive what you send, such as your name, email address, phone number and message. I use it only to reply to you and to deliver work you ask for.

2. Data accessed through Google

Some automations connect to Google services when you sign in with Google and approve access. Depending on the automation, this can include:

  • Gmail: reading new emails that match the search you set (for example, enquiries), replying to them, sending you an alert email, and marking them as read.
  • Google Sheets: adding a row for each new lead to a spreadsheet you choose.

This data is processed by the automation running in your own n8n (on your computer, your server or your n8n Cloud account). It is used only to run the automation you set up. AutomaBuild does not copy it to its own servers, sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

AutomaBuild's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How long data is kept

Emails stay in your Gmail and lead rows stay in your Google Sheet until you delete them. Messages you send me directly are kept only as long as needed to help you, then deleted on request.

4. Your choices

  • You can stop the automation at any time by deactivating it in n8n.
  • You can remove Google access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps & services.
  • You can ask me to delete anything you sent me by emailing the address below.

5. Security

Google sign-ins use Google's OAuth system, so your Google password is never shared with AutomaBuild. Access keys stay inside your own n8n.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

7. Contact

Questions or requests: hi@automabuild.com
