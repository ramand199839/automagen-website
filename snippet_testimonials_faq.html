Everything you need to know before we start building together.

Business automation connects your existing tools — CRMs, email, spreadsheets, forms, databases — so they talk to each other and act on your behalf without manual input. Think of it as a digital employee that runs 24/7: it watches for triggers (a new lead, a payment, a form submission) and executes a sequence of actions (send an email, update a record, notify your Slack). We design these workflows using platforms like Make, n8n, Zapier, and custom code where needed.

Most single-workflow automations are scoped, built, tested, and handed over within 5–10 business days. More complex multi-system integrations or AI-powered pipelines typically take 2–4 weeks. We move fast because we start with a focused discovery call to nail down requirements before writing a single line of logic — no endless back-and-forth.
