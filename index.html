Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start building together.

Business automation connects your existing tools — CRMs, email, spreadsheets, forms, databases — so they talk to each other and act on your behalf without manual input. Think of it as a digital employee that runs 24/7: it watches for triggers (a new lead, a payment, a form submission) and executes a sequence of actions (send an email, update a record, notify your Slack). We design these workflows using platforms like Make, n8n, Zapier, and custom code where needed.

Most single-workflow automations are scoped, built, tested, and handed over within 5–10 business days. More complex multi-system integrations or AI-powered pipelines typically take 2–4 weeks. We move fast because we start with a focused discovery session to nail down requirements before writing a single line of logic.

We offer both. For clearly scoped, one-off builds we charge a flat project fee agreed upfront — no surprises. For ongoing support, new automations, or iterative improvements, we offer monthly retainer plans. We'll recommend the model that makes more sense after understanding your situation.

We're tool-agnostic and choose based on your needs. Our primary stack includes Make (Integromat), n8n, Zapier, Airtable, and custom Python/Node.js scripts for logic that platforms can't handle. For AI features we leverage the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs. We never lock you into a tool you don't already own.

Yes. Every project includes a 14-day post-launch support window at no extra cost — if anything misfires in the real world, we fix it. Beyond that, retainer clients get priority support, proactive monitoring, and monthly improvement sessions.

Automations can break when third-party tools update their APIs — it's normal. For retainer clients, we monitor critical workflows and fix issues proactively. For project-only clients, we provide full documentation so you can locate and patch issues quickly. We also build in error-handling alerts so you're always notified if something stops working.
